Recover your old FORM journal

This page reads workout journals saved in this browser at the old address, localhost:4173. It does not change or delete them and does not access calendar data or Firebase.

After downloading, close this recovery terminal. Start FORM at localhost:4174/form/, open its account panel and choose Import backup in the journal you want to use.

Keep your downloaded backups. If you used another browser, another profile, or 127.0.0.1 instead of localhost, open this recovery page there too.
